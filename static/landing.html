Relational Fluency a workplace conversation practice platform
What it is Skills Researcher
A research demo

Practice the hard conversations at work, before they happen.

Talk with AI coworkers, managers, and teammates in realistic workplace situations. Give difficult feedback. Repair after you snap at someone. Run a tense meeting where one person is shut down and another is taking over. The AI plays the other person, with their own personality and history. They push back, open up, or go quiet based on how you show up.

Researcher view

Why this exists

You only get to have the real conversation once.

When something matters at work, like giving someone hard feedback or apologizing for a public mistake, you rarely get a chance to rehearse. This platform gives you that chance.

Four skills the platform measures

What gets stressed in each conversation.
01

Perspective taking

Drawing out what the other person actually thinks, not the easy surface answer.

02

Emotional regulation

Staying useful when you are frustrated, blindsided, or being criticized.

03

Apology and repair

Owning a mistake cleanly. Not crumbling. Not deflecting.

04

Psychological safety

Making it safe for the other person to be honest, especially when they have less power than you.
