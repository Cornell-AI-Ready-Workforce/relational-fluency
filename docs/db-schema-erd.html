Overview

Every table and every foreign key, without columns. Group 1 is reference data; groups 2 to 4 are loaded from the S3 archive; group 5 from Qualtrics exports; group 6 is where step 2 writes; groups 7 and 8 are not produced yet.

1 · Scenario bank

The instrument: the eight forms (S1A to S4B), their casts and actor briefs, the two interactions each, and the planted triggers with their high and low anchors. ESCI items key on the construct because parallel forms share them.

Loaded from scenarios/v3/*.yaml

  • construct PK construct
  • esci_item PK construct, item_key
  • scenario PK scenario_id
  • scenario_agent PK scenario_id, agent_id
  • interaction PK scenario_id, interaction_id
  • planted_trigger PK scenario_id, trigger_id

2 · People and runs

One row per participant, with the CloudResearch key split into a restricted identity table. A run is the four-slot assignment with its arm and Williams row; slots exist whether or not the encounter happened. Withdrawal is the stop-button stamp.

Loaded from data/runs/<run_id>.json, data/participants/<pid>.json

  • participant PK participant_id
  • participant_identity PK participant_id
  • run PK run_id
  • run_slot PK run_id, slot
  • withdrawal PK run_id

3 · Encounters

The conversation as recorded: every turn on both sides, each stage direction paired to the reply it produced, each planted trigger as it fired or was retracted, who the director chose to speak, and the raw event trail. Provenance and integrity counts sit on the encounter row.

Loaded from manifest.json, record.json, events.jsonl

  • encounter PK encounter_id
  • encounter_cast PK encounter_id, agent_id
  • turn PK turn_id
  • stage_direction PK direction_id
  • trigger_firing PK firing_id
  • director_route PK encounter_id, t
  • event PK event_id

4 · Media and transcripts

Pointers to the audio and video objects in S3 (files never go in the database) and the alternative transcripts: live, offline re-transcription of the participant channel, and dialogue recovered from video.

Loaded from record.json, S3 listing, recovered_transcript.md

  • media PK media_id
  • transcript_version PK transcript_id

5 · Surveys

Qualtrics Survey 1 and Survey 2 responses, joined to the run by ResponseID and by the run id and completion code piped back. Answers stay JSON until instrument scoring is decided.

Loaded from python -m server.qualtrics export / join

  • survey PK survey_id
  • survey_response PK response_id

6 · Ratings and scores step 2 writes here

Phase 2: which rater watched which encounter, one score per ESCI item with the turns they pointed at. Phase 3: the model scorer's item scores, versioned so runs can be compared with the human ratings. Step 2 (the rater app) writes the first three tables.

Loaded from step 2 rater app (planned); Phase 3 scorer

  • rater PK rater_id
  • rating_assignment PK assignment_id
  • rating PK rating_id
  • model_score PK score_id

7 · RCT

Phase 4: the arm per participant, and what feedback was delivered when. Second attempts live on run.attempt_number and run.sibling_run_id.

Loaded from not yet produced

  • rct_assignment PK participant_id
  • feedback_delivery PK delivery_id

8 · Operations

Task-definition revisions with their image and models, so an analysis can bracket encounters by what was running.

Loaded from ECS task-definition history

  • deployment PK revision

9 · Views

The three questions asked most often, kept as views so analysts do not re-derive them.

  • v_encounter_summary: one row per encounter: scenario, construct, models, turn counts, triggers fired against planned, whether video exists
  • v_participant_progress: one row per run: encounters done, completed, has survey 1 and 2, withdrawn_at
  • v_esci_coverage: per encounter: the ESCI items the stage directions exercised against the items in the construct
